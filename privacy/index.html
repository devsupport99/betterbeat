私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 11 日

中文English

簡單講：你的紀錄只存在你自己的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上。我們沒有伺服器、沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤，也看不到你的任何資料。

1. 我們收集甚麼資料

開發者不會收集、接收或儲存你的任何個人資料。App 沒有連接任何伺服器，也沒有使用任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤工具。

2. App 在你的裝置上處理甚麼資料

Better Beat 是一個男士私密紀錄 App。為了記錄每一次，App 會在你的裝置上處理以下資料：

  • 動作感測器數據（加速度計、陀螺儀）：在 Apple Watch 上用來計算頻率、次數、幅度和停頓。
  • 心率：經 Apple「健康」（HealthKit）讀取，只在你授權後才會讀取。
  • 睡眠：只有你在設定中開啟「睡眠分析」並授權後，才會從 Apple「健康」讀取，用來比較睡前有紀錄和其他晚上。睡眠數據只在分析時讀取，App 不會儲存副本。
  • 紀錄摘要：每一次的時間、時長、頻率、幅度（估算）、心率變化、用哪隻手等。原始感測器數據只在記錄期間用於計算，不會儲存。
  • 設定：例如你慣用哪隻手。

這些資料只儲存在你的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上的 App 內，並透過 Apple 的連接功能在你自己的兩部裝置之間傳送。分析功能完全在你的 iPhone 上計算。

3. Apple「健康」資料

  • App 只會讀取心率和（如你開啟睡眠分析）睡眠數據，不會寫入任何資料到「健康」或「健身」App。
  • 從「健康」取得的資料不會用於廣告或推銷，不會出售，也不會與任何第三方分享。
  • 你可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定」>「私隱與保安」>「健康」中更改或撤回授權。

4. 備份

App 內的紀錄不會包含在 iCloud 備份或電腦備份中，只存在這部 iPhone 上。因此更換手機時，紀錄不會轉移到新手機。

5. 購買

Pro 訂閱和購買由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。我們不會收到你的姓名、付款資料或 Apple 帳號資料，只會從 Apple 得知你是否擁有 Pro。

6. 刪除資料

  • 你可以在「紀錄」中左滑刪除任何一次紀錄，或在「設定」中刪除全部紀錄。
  • 刪除 App 會移除裝置上所有相關資料。

7. 年齡

本 App 只供 18 歲或以上人士使用。

8. 健康聲明

  • 非醫療用途：Better Beat 僅供個人記錄及一般參考，並非醫療器材，亦不提供任何醫療、診斷、治療或預防疾病的建議。
  • 數據為估算值：頻率、次數、心率及睡眠等數據由 Apple Watch 感測器估算，可能因佩戴方式、動作及裝置狀況而有誤差，不應作為任何醫療判斷的依據。
  • 分析不代表因果：分析及洞察只描述你個人紀錄中的數據規律，不代表因果關係，亦不構成任何健康或行為建議。
  • 尋求專業意見：如對自身健康、性健康或心理狀況有任何疑問，或感到身體不適，請諮詢合資格的醫療專業人員。如遇緊急情況，請立即聯絡當地緊急服務。

9. 政策更新

如本政策有更新，我們會在此頁面更新日期。重大更改會在 App 更新說明中提及。

10. 聯絡我們

如有任何私隱問題，請電郵至 betterbeat.support@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: your sessions stay on your own iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no server, no account, no ads and no analytics tracking, and we never see any of your data.

1. What we collect

The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your personal data. The app does not connect to any server and uses no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. What the app processes on your device

  • Motion sensor data (accelerometer, gyroscope), used on Apple Watch to work out rate, count, amplitude and pauses.
  • Heart rate, read from Apple Health (HealthKit) only after you allow it.
  • Sleep, read from Apple Health only if you turn on Sleep Analysis in Settings and allow it, to compare nights with and without a session beforehand. Sleep data is read when analysing; the app keeps no copy.
  • Session summaries (time, duration, rate, estimated amplitude, heart-rate changes, which hand). Raw sensor data is used only to calculate these during a session and is not stored.
  • Settings, such as which hand you usually use.

This data is stored only inside the app on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and moves between your own two devices using Apple's connectivity features. Analysis is computed entirely on your iPhone.

3. Apple Health data

  • The app only reads heart rate and, if you turn on Sleep Analysis, sleep. It writes nothing to the Health or Fitness app.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

4. Backups

The app's records are not included in iCloud or computer backups; they stay on this iPhone only. This means they don't move to a new phone.

5. Purchases

Pro subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account details; we only learn from Apple whether you have Pro.

6. Deleting your data

  • You can swipe to delete any session in the history list, or delete all records in Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your devices.

7. Age

This app is intended only for people aged 18 or over.

8. Health disclaimer

  • Not medical: Better Beat is for personal record-keeping and general reference only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or prevention of any condition.
  • Estimates: rate, count, heart rate and sleep figures are estimated by Apple Watch sensors and may be inaccurate depending on fit, movement and device conditions. They should not be relied on for any medical decision.
  • No causation: analysis and insights describe patterns in your own records only. They do not show cause and effect and are not health or behavioural advice.
  • Seek professional advice: if you have any questions about your health, sexual health or mental wellbeing, or feel unwell, consult a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email betterbeat.support@gmail.com.
